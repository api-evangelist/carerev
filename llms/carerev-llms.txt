# CareRev

> CareRev is a healthcare workforce management and on-demand labor marketplace that connects hospitals and health systems directly with local, credentialed clinical professionals — registered nurses, LPN/LVNs, CNAs, surgical and ER techs, medical assistants, respiratory therapists, phlebotomists and mental health techs — without a traditional staffing agency in the middle. Facilities post open shifts from their existing scheduling and HR systems, and vetted clinicians browse, claim and self-schedule them in the CareRev app. CareRev is certified by The Joint Commission under its Health Care Staffing Services program and serves more than 650 US facilities.

Generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline on 2026-08-02 from apis.yml and the artifacts in this repository. CareRev does not publish its own /llms.txt.

## API status

CareRev publishes **no public API program**: no developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI/Swagger/GraphQL/AsyncAPI contract, no SDKs, no sandbox and no self-serve credentials. `api.carerev.com` is a real, live API gateway (Istio/Envoy, HTTP 204 at root, HSTS enforced) serving the CareRev clinician and facility applications and CareRev's partner integrations, but it is auth-gated and undocumented. Integration is arranged through a CareRev partnership or customer implementation — CareRev's partners page describes API connections into a facility's VMS, scheduling and HR stack, including UKG Dimensions via the UKG Connect Technology Partner Program.

## Products

- [CareRev Marketplace](https://www.carerev.com/solutions/carerev-marketplace): On-demand access to local, vetted, credentialed clinical professionals for shift coverage.
- [CareRev IRP+](https://www.carerev.com/solutions/carerev-irp): Internal resource pool and float-pool control, visibility and forecasting for a health system's own staff.
- [Smart Rates](https://www.carerev.com/solutions/smart-rates-healthcare-staffing): Dynamic rate modeling to forecast demand, model cost and control labor spend.
